1. Clinical Need & Motivation
Problem Statement & Clinical Motivation
RUBRIC COMPONENT 1 (5 MARKS)
The Clinical Problem
Acoustic ASR Fails on Dysarthria
  • Neuromotor disorders (Cerebral Palsy, ALS, Stroke) cause dysarthria—impaired vocal articulation and distorted acoustics.
  • Commercial acoustic ASR (Whisper, Siri, Alexa) suffers >60–80% Word Error Rates.
  • Acoustic models fail in noise or when vocal cord phonation is physically lost.
Why It Matters
Silent Lip-Reading as Assistive AAC
  • Visual Speech Recognition (VSR) decodes speech entirely from visual mouth kinematics in complete silence.
  • Completely immune to acoustic background noise and vocal cord degradation.
  • Enables hands-free emergency alerts and smart device control for non-vocal users.
The Research Gap
Why Existing VSR Models Collapse
  • Kinematic Distortion: Standard models assume healthy, symmetric lip motion. Dysarthric speakers have spastic drift and reduced motion amplitude.
  • Physical Fatigue: Patients cannot record 50 hours of video. Models must adapt in ≤ 5 examples.
  • Catastrophic Overfitting: Full fine-tuning on 5 clips destroys pretrained representations.

4. Technical Innovation
Novelty, Innovation & Team Contributions
RUBRIC COMPONENT 2 (5 MARKS)
Innovation 01 | Parameter Efficiency
Bottleneck Residual Adapter Tuning
Instead of destructive full fine-tuning (12.5M weights), we freeze 99.2% of the network and insert residual bottleneck adapters (512 → 64 → 512). Restricting gradient updates to ~100K weights prevents catastrophic forgetting on tiny data budgets (≤ 5 clips).
Innovation 02 | Non-Parametric Adaptation
Cosine Prototype Matching
Constructed a zero-gradient adaptation mode: class centroids are computed in BiGRU embedding space from support shots, enabling instant 1-shot personalization with cosine similarity and zero backpropagation steps.
Innovation 03 | Evaluation Rigor
LOBO Folds & Exact Clopper-Pearson CIs
Eliminated the common practice of reporting overfitted random clip splits. We enforce Leave-One-Block-Out cross-validation across independent recording sessions and report exact binomial Clopper-Pearson confidence intervals.
Innovation 04 | Live AAC System
Hardware-Decoupled Webcam Pipeline
Solved the lab-to-webcam domain gap via background-threaded 25.0 FPS timestamp resampling and statistical luminance matching (μ=148, σ=27), preventing dead-zone ReLU collapse on indoor webcams.
5. Experimental Data Curation
Dataset Details: GRID vs. UASpeech
STUDENT 1: MODULE 1 (3.5 MINS)
Source Domain: GRID Corpus
Typical Laboratory Speech Benchmark
  • Speakers: 8 neurotypical speakers (s1–s8).
  • Total Video Clips: 7,859 preprocessed clips (75 frames × 50 × 100 grayscale).
  • Total Words: 47,154 word occurrences across 6 syntactic slots.
  • Vocabulary: 51 unique words (commands, colors, letters, digits, adverbs).
  • Split Strategy: 90% pretraining (7,073 clips), 10% test (786 clips). Gate test holds out s7 & s8 as strictly unseen speakers (5,984 train clips).
Target Domain: UASpeech Video
Pathological Speech (Cerebral Palsy)
  • Participants: F05 (Mild, 95% intelligibility) and F02 (Severe, 29% intelligibility).
  • Recording Structure: 3 separate recording blocks (B1, B2, B3) per speaker.
  • Segmented Clips: 765 word clips for F05; 509 word clips for F02.
  • Evaluation Classes: 10 isolated digits (0–9) for controlled few-shot testing; 155 repeated vocabulary words.
  • Cross-Validation: Strict Leave-One-Block-Out (LOBO) folds. Zero session overlap.
6. Data Preprocessing Pipeline
Mouth ROI Extraction, Resampling & Leakage Prevention
STUDENT 1: MODULE 1
Stabilization
Median Bounding Box Fix
  • Failure Mode: Frame-by-frame face detection caused bounding box jitter, destroying optical flow and freezing CTC loss at ln 4 = 1.386.
  • Solution: Sample 8 frames across the clip and compute the MEDIAN bounding box [x, y, w, h].
  • Mouth subregion: lower 35%–90% of face box, resized to 50 × 100.
Temporal Scaling
Resampling vs. Padding
  • Isolated words vary from 15 to 45 frames. LipNet requires 75 frames (3.0s @ 25 FPS).
  • Linear Resampling: Achieves 32.6% Top-1.
  • Zero-Padding: Drops to 16.8% Top-1 because zero padding corrupts temporal BatchNorm statistics.
Integrity
Zero Data Leakage
  • Disjoint Sentences: Support and query sentences for typical control evaluations are strictly disjoint.
  • Session Isolation: Adaptation support clips and test clips are drawn from completely different recording blocks.
  • No Peeking: Normalization parameters are calculated strictly per-clip or from support blocks.

8. Deep Learning Methodology
LipNet Architecture & Exact Training Hyperparameters
STUDENT 2: MODULE 2
Neural Architecture Details
3D-CNN Frontend + BiGRU Backend
  • Input Tensor: (B, 1, 75, 50, 100) — Grayscale mouth ROI.
  • Conv3D Block 1: 32 filters, (3,5,5) kernel → BN → ReLU → MaxPool3D → Dropout3D(0.4).
  • Conv3D Block 2: 64 filters, (3,5,5) kernel → BN → ReLU → MaxPool3D → Dropout3D(0.4).
  • Conv3D Block 3: 96 filters, (3,3,3) kernel → BN → ReLU → MaxPool3D → Dropout3D(0.4).
  • Spatial Features: 96 × 6 × 12 = 6,912 features per frame.
  • BiGRU Backend: 2 layers, 256 hidden units/dir = 512 bidirectional features.
  • CTC Head: Linear(512 → 52) [51 words + 1 CTC blank].

Key Empirical Insights
Full Fine-Tune vs. Adapter Tuning
  • Catastrophic Overfitting: Full fine-tuning on k=1–5 clips causes Top-1 accuracy to collapse (from 20% to 8% on F05) due to gradient collapse.
  • Adapter Stability: Bottleneck adapter preserves pretrained representations, maintaining stability on mild speech.
  • Top-3 Gain on Severe Dysarthria (F02): On F02 (29% speech intelligibility), adapter tuning lifts Top-3 accuracy from 10% up to 40.0% at k=10.
  • 590% WER Artifact Resolution: Sentence CTC decoders hallucinate insertion tokens on isolated words; constrained sequence decoding bounds WER in [0%, 100%].

13. Conclusions & Next Steps
Limitations, Future Scope & Project Summary
RUBRIC COMPONENT 5 (20 MARKS)
Honest Limitations
Current Constraints
  • Viseme Ambiguity: Phonemes with identical lip mechanics (e.g. /p/, /b/, /m/) produce identical visual patterns.
  • Spastic Head Motion: Involuntary spasms reduce Haar face detection from 100% (GRID) to 87.1% (F05 Block B1).
  • Cohort Size: UASpeech public video is restricted to a small cohort.
Future Roadmap
Engineering Next Steps
  • LoRA on BiGRU Gates: Inject low-rank decomposition matrices (r=4) into recurrent gate weights.
  • AdaBN Normalization: Recalculate BatchNorm statistics on unlabeled user video to align feature distributions.
  • Sensor Fusion: Combine lip-reading with surface electromyography (sEMG) or throat microphones.
Core Contributions
Key Takeaways
  • Demonstrated that sentence pretraining yields chance on isolated words; word cuts restore typical accuracy to 30%.
  • Proved bottleneck adapters prevent catastrophic forgetting, lifting Top-3 accuracy on severe dysarthria to 40%.
  • Delivered a real-time, privacy-preserving desktop application that empowers motor-impaired individuals.
